How information is handled when you play Animal Carnival.

This Privacy Policy explains how MPMF Game (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you download, install, or play Animal Carnival (“the Game”). Where required by applicable law, we will request consent before optional analytics or advertising-related processing.

1. Information we handle

Gameplay and app activity

Depending on the version of the Game and services enabled, information may include installation and launch events, sessions, level starts and results, progress and unlocks, virtual coins, outfit selections, and technical events used to understand errors and event delivery. Animal Carnival contains short competitive mini-games with different rules; event details, where collected, relate to those games rather than weapons, headshots, or other features of a different title.

Device and technical information

We and enabled service providers may process app version, device model, operating system, language or region, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, network information, performance and diagnostic data, and device or advertising identifiers where permitted.

Information stored on your device

The Game may store save data locally, such as level unlocks, coins, outfits, settings, and progress. Clearing app data or uninstalling the Game may remove local saves unless your device or platform provides a backup.

Information you send us

If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and any screenshots or diagnostic details you choose to share.

2. How we use information

We use information as needed to operate the Game, retain and display progress, respond to support and privacy requests, understand how mini-games are used, improve performance and balance, investigate errors or abuse, and—where enabled—measure app installations and the effectiveness of advertising campaigns. We will provide additional notice or obtain consent when required for a materially different use.

3. Adjust analytics and attribution

Animal Carnival includes an Adjust integration. When configured and enabled in a release, Adjust may process installation, launch, session, in-game event, device, IP-address, identifier, attribution, and diagnostic information for analytics, attribution, advertising measurement, and fraud prevention. Event parameters may describe mini-game level identifiers, results, progress, virtual coins, or unlocks. The exact events depend on the released version.

Adjust’s handling is described in its Privacy Policy. The presence of an integration in the Game does not mean every possible event or identifier is collected.

3A. Planned Meta / Facebook SDK

We plan to integrate the Meta (formerly Facebook) SDK in a future release. This section describes processing that may begin only if the SDK is actually integrated and enabled; it does not mean the current version sends information to Meta.

When enabled, Meta may receive installation, launch, session, and selected in-game event information, together with app version, device and operating-system details, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, and advertising or other permitted identifiers. These data may be used to measure advertising performance, attribute installs and events, provide aggregated analytics, and help detect fraud. Access to an advertising identifier or other tracking will follow applicable device permissions and consent requirements. The SDK does not give us access to your Facebook password or private messages. See the Meta Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing and third-party services

Information may be processed by providers that support analytics or attribution, app distribution, diagnostics, security, or customer support, only as relevant to a feature or service in use. Adjust is described above; Meta would become a recipient only after its planned SDK is enabled in a released version. The app store or operating-system provider may handle information under its own privacy policy when you install, update, or use the Game. We may also disclose information when legally required, to protect users and our rights, or in connection with a business transfer subject to applicable law.

We do not directly sell personal information for money. Some advertising measurement or attribution activities may be treated as “sharing,” “sale,” or targeted advertising under certain laws; the applicable choices are described below.

5. Consent and device choices

Where consent is required for optional analytics or advertising measurement, we will request it through the Game or applicable platform controls. You may also use your device’s privacy settings to limit or reset advertising identifiers. Declining optional tracking should not prevent access to the Game’s core play. Some processing required to operate the Game or respond to requests may continue as permitted by law.

6. Retention, security, and international processing

We retain information only as reasonably necessary for the purposes above, security, dispute resolution, or legal requirements. Local save data remains on your device until it is changed, cleared, or removed. Service providers may have separate retention practices. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no transmission or storage method is completely secure. Providers may process information outside your country; where required, appropriate legal safeguards apply.

7. Your privacy rights and deletion requests

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, or restriction of personal information; object to certain processing; withdraw consent; or opt out of certain advertising-related sharing. You may also complain to a relevant data-protection authority. Contact xiongchang@mpmfgame.com with the subject “Animal Carnival Privacy Request.” For deletion, use “Animal Carnival Data Deletion Request.” Include enough information to identify your request, but do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive data. We may verify your identity and retain information where legally required.

8. Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable child-privacy laws. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child provided information without legally required authorization, contact us. We will review the request and take appropriate action.

9. Changes and third-party links

The Game may link to third-party services governed by their own policies. We may update this policy when the Game, its services, or legal requirements change. We will revise the “Last updated” date and give additional notice or seek consent when required.
